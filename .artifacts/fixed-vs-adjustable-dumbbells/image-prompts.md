# Normalized image-generation prompt record

All requests used the same non-negotiable constraints: original photorealistic editorial image, 3:2 horizontal composition, fictional unbranded gym showroom, a complete fixed-dumbbell rack on one side, exactly two selectorized adjustable dumbbells resting in separate bases on the other, clear visual comparison, no people, no logos, no brand geometry, no readable text or numbers, no watermarks, no charts, no claim that the scene is a real factory or customer project, and enough calm negative space for a website hero crop.

Locale-specific art direction:

- **en:** premium neutral commercial showroom; charcoal rubber floor, soft daylight, balanced straight-on composition, realistic steel and rubber surfaces.
- **pt-BR:** bright contemporary club interior; warm daylight, restrained green and blue accents, wider aisle, energetic but credible hospitality setting.
- **es:** sunlit modern gym; warm stone and muted terracotta accents, inviting central aisle, clean equipment separation.
- **de:** precise minimalist studio; cool daylight, graphite and pale wood palette, orderly architectural lines and generous operating clearance.
- **fr:** refined boutique training room; soft side light, warm grey and understated navy accents, elegant but operational rather than decorative.
- **vi:** compact urban fitness room; humid daylight feel, muted teal accents, space-efficient plan with clear access to both systems.
- **sv:** Scandinavian training space; pale timber, cool diffuse daylight, restrained monochrome palette, simple open circulation.
- **it:** contemporary Mediterranean studio; warm natural light, soft mineral walls, dark equipment and uncluttered premium finish.
- **nl:** crisp practical sports-club interior; large-window daylight, neutral flooring, honest functional layout and broad circulation.
- **ar:** upscale modern hotel fitness room; warm stone tones, subtle geometric architecture, polished but unbranded equipment, calm side light.
- **ko:** compact high-finish urban gym; cool neutral lighting, minimal steel-and-grey palette, precise symmetrical arrangement, no selector markings.
- **id:** bright tropical hospitality gym; filtered daylight, muted green accents, durable dark floor and clear compact circulation.
- **pl:** robust contemporary strength room; cool industrial daylight, concrete-grey backdrop, orderly steel rack and clean bases, no selector markings.
