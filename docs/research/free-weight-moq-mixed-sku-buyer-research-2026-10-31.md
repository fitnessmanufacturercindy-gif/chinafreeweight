# Free-weight MOQ and mixed-SKU buyer research — 2026-10-31

## Selected buyer problem

Wholesale buyers often receive an MOQ without knowing its scope. The useful question is not only “What is the MOQ?” but whether the minimum applies to the whole purchase order, each SKU or weight, a shared material or colour batch, a logo process, retail packaging, or a shipment plan.

This topic was selected because it has clear procurement intent and was not covered by the existing multilingual library. The nearest published topics concern gym-use dumbbell-set planning, RFQ preparation, GTIN and packaging, and landed-cost planning; none provides a single matrix for separating product, customization, packaging, and logistics constraints.

## Questions observed

- Is the dumbbell minimum quoted by piece, pair, set, or production mass?
- Can different dumbbell weights or plate sizes share one minimum?
- Does a total container order remove every per-SKU constraint?
- Why can product, custom-logo, and private-label packaging minimums differ?
- Which RFQ fields let a manufacturer evaluate a mixed assortment accurately?
- How should a buyer handle slow-moving sizes, repeat orders, and unused packaging?
- Which exceptions and assumptions should be written into the purchase order?

The same intent was localized independently for English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Vietnamese, Swedish, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Korean, Indonesian, and Polish procurement language.
