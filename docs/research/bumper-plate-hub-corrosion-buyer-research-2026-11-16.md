## Evidence boundaries

- [ISO 9227:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/81744.html) defines salt-spray apparatus and procedure; it does not set a bumper-plate exposure, pass threshold or field-life prediction.
- [ASTM B117-26](https://store.astm.org/standards/b117) likewise describes the apparatus and cautions against treating a stand-alone result as a natural-service prediction.
- [ASTM A380/A380M](https://store.astm.org/a0380_a0380m-17.html) was reviewed for free-iron contamination and purchaser-supplier agreement vocabulary, not for a public cleaning recipe.
- [BSSA guidance](https://bssa.org.uk/bssa_articles/iron-contamination-and-rust-staining-on-stainless-steel/) supports the fact that external iron contamination can rust-stain stainless surfaces.
- [Nickel Institute handling guidance](https://nickelinstitute.org/media/2898/erectioninstallation.pdf) supports preventing carbon-steel contamination during storage and transport.
- A [US DLA procurement description](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/Transient/38350E182FAA4BD491F0D84034C2A438.pdf) was used only to show that a buyer may specify a stainless insert. It is not presented as a universal rule.
