## Primary technical references

1. REP hex dumbbell specifications: https://repfitness.com/products/rubber-hex-dumbbell-pairs — identifies friction welding for that product.
2. Ivanko IUDB specifications: https://ivankobarbell.com/pages/urethane-iudb-urethane-dumbbells — describes a non-welded, mechanically fastened construction. This is why the article does not claim every non-welded design is inferior.
3. Iron Grip dumbbell construction: https://irongrip.com/dumbbells-barbells/ — describes a combined press-fit and welded arrangement. This prevents treating press fit and welding as mutually exclusive labels.
4. Vulcan Elite Hex official page: https://www.vulcanstrength.com/Vulcan-Elite-Hex-Rubber-Dumbbells-p/hxdx-6.htm — identifies an interference-fit approach for a particular model.
5. Iron Grip care guide: https://irongrip.com/care-guide/ — calls for periodic equipment inspection and describes risks from repeated dropping. Care chemicals and warranty terms were not copied into a universal recommendation.
6. Rogue rubber hex official page: https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-dumbbells — describes a proprietary head-to-handle construction without publishing enough detail to infer every internal manufacturing step.

The article's testing and purchasing framework is an editorial recommendation, not a claimed mandatory standard, certified test method, universal threshold or PowerBaseFit performance result. Each published edition links to primary model specifications while making their limited applicability explicit.
