# Private-label free-weight GTIN and carton-label research — 2026-10-28

## Selected buyer problem

Brand owners, importers and distributors need a reliable handoff between the product record they control and the label that a factory prints or applies. The chosen guide separates the identifier, the barcode symbol, the buyer's internal SKU and the packaging level. It also covers single items, pairs, sets, inner packs and master cartons without inventing channel-specific rules.

The subject was selected because the existing multilingual library already covers logo methods, export packaging, sample approval and pre-shipment inspection, but did not contain a dedicated guide to identifier ownership, item hierarchy or label-file control. The live inventory reviewed for the run contained 901 blog URLs and no dedicated GTIN or barcode article.

## Buyer questions observed

- Should the brand owner or the factory obtain the UPC or EAN?
- Does every dumbbell weight or plate variant need a separate GTIN?
- Can the same identifier be used for a single item, a pair, a set and a case?
- Is an internal SKU the same thing as a GTIN?
- What does the buyer need to send with an approved label file?
- Where should a barcode be placed on a retail carton or master carton?
- What should be checked on a printed sample before bulk packing?
- How should repeat orders control identifier and artwork revisions?

The wording came from focused Google and Bing result review, People Also Ask-style questions, Reddit buyer discussions, industry pages and private-label supplier pages. Quora did not permit automated access, so no answer text from it was used. Reddit was used only to understand buyer phrasing; standards claims rely on GS1 sources.

## Primary references

- [GS1: Who is responsible for numbering trade items?](https://support.gs1.org/support/solutions/articles/43000734414-who-is-responsible-for-numbering-trade-items-) — the normal responsibility of the brand owner, including an importer or wholesaler selling under its own brand.
- [GS1 GTIN Management Standard](https://www.gs1.org/sites/default/files/docs/barcodes/GS1_GTIN_Management_Standard.pdf) — review triggers such as a new product, declared content, brand and pack or case quantity changes.
- [GS1: barcode quality checks](https://support.gs1.org/support/solutions/articles/43000734141-what-should-i-check-to-ensure-good-quality-barcodes-) — check digit, quiet zones, contrast, height, substrate, damage and placement.
- [GS1 General Specifications](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/) — placement guidance and the need to avoid seams, folds, edges, staples and conflicting visible identifiers.
- [Reddit buyer discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/barcodes/comments/1arou7a/questions_about_barcodes_from_a_soon_to_be_food/) — natural questions about brand-versus-manufacturer responsibility and applying an approved code at an overseas factory.

## Competitive and conflict review

GymWeightSupply, DumbbellManufacturers and Yunlingyu mention barcode labels or carton files as private-label inputs. Their commercial claims were not reused. The new guide instead provides a decision framework: ownership, hierarchy, artwork handoff, print checks and receiving rehearsal.

Nearest ChinaFreeWeight topics checked were custom-logo free weights, export packaging, OEM sample approval and pre-shipment inspection. The new titles, descriptions, H1s, slugs and primary terms are unique in every locale. The 2026-10-27 weight-plate storage intent was deliberately excluded.

## Claim boundaries

- No customer, project, quantity, price, minimum order, lead time, certification, test result or sales outcome is asserted.
- ChinaFreeWeight is described as the factory that can receive approved buyer data, prepare a print proof, apply labels and check the physical result. The buyer or brand owner remains responsible for its identifiers and channel requirements.
- No universal marketplace, retailer or legal rule is stated. Buyers are told to confirm the current requirements of their destination and sales channel.
- Generated visuals are editorial still lifes of unbranded free-weight products, neutral cartons and abstract label proofs. They are not presented as a customer project or factory record.

## Localization approach

Each of the 13 editions uses local purchasing language and channel context rather than sentence-level translation. Examples include marca própria and caixa máster in Brazil, Eigenmarke and Artikelstamm in DACH, BAT and logisticien in France, artikelregister and ytterkartong in Sweden, hàng nhãn riêng and thùng ngoài in Vietnam, and locally natural equivalents for piece, pair, set and master carton in Arabic and Korean.
